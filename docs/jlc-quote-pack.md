## Universal order checklist

Manifold STL, mm units, min-wall per process table, no enclosed
hollows (WJP), drainage if hollow SLA, text ≥ process minimums,
tolerance callouts on mating features, finish stated explicitly,
quantity + ship-to with order. Keep every quote (list → landed)
— they become the cost anchors for the whole catalogue.
