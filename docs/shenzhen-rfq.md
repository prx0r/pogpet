# Elecrow revisit — RFQ draft (expands the Sep 24 Glimling enquiry)

Prior contact: 2026-09-24 re Glimling (custom 3D parts, third-party
electronics sourcing, assembly, firmware load + test, custom packaging,
Shopify fulfilment, worldwide dropshipping; M5Stack Atom Voice + HHCC
Flower Care sensor core). This draft expands that spec from electronic
gifts to mixed-material personalised project kits.

## What to ask (one RFQ, three scenarios)

Reference the September enquiry ID, then the expanded brief:

1. **10 preassembled kits** (fixed recipe: JLC resin figure + 1688
   beads/chain + printed booklet + branded box) — all-in per kit.
2. **10 personalised kits** (same recipe, unique booklet + one
   made-to-order JLC part each) — all-in per kit + booklet-print method.
3. **1 bespoke order** (custom JLC part + mixed craft materials) — full
   handling chain cost.

For each: receiving (multi-supplier, our components accepted?),
inspection method, storage fees, assembly steps, booklet printing
(unique 8–16pp per order: yes/no/how), packaging options + box MOQs,
single-parcel dispatch, tracking, UK delivery cost + time, duties
handling, QC photos/audit option, firmware load + test (for electronic
variants), defect/returns handling.

## Decision gates

- Single-order made-to-order JLC parts alongside stocked materials +
  unique booklets (the capability that unlocks personalised at scale).
- Mixed non-electronic craft materials accepted (paper, thread, beads,
  bottles) + safety posture for each.
- Makerfabs quoted in parallel on the same three scenarios; Seeed where
  electronics dominate. Pick on delivered cost + audit quality, not
  headline kitting rates.
