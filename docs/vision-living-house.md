# The Living House — a physical home for your AI companion

## The thesis

The house is the robot. No legs, arms or humanoid face needed: the
inhabitant exists in imagination, speaks through the house, appears in
its digital counterpart on demand. Unlike a smart speaker, the house has
a place in your life, a recognisable inhabitant, and a physical form you
can gradually customise.

## The experience

You buy Jenny's Cottage, a fairy house with a warm upstairs window. At
night: "Jenny, wake me at seven tomorrow and read me something before I
fall asleep." — "Seven? That's ambitious. All right. I'll find something
good." Windows dim, book-opening sound, rain, Jenny reading in her voice;
optionally see her in the armchair by the fire on your phone. Morning:
window brightens, Jenny speaks the wake-up. Continuous character across
alarm, audiobook and chat — not three apps. Alarm stored and executed by
a reliable scheduler or local clock, never entrusted to an LLM's memory.

## Modularity (the commercial engine)

01 The House (decorative cottage + identity + digital counterpart) ·
02 Light Module (fades, sunrise, status) · 03 Voice Module (mic + speaker,
no app needed) · 04 Window Screen (resident reading/painting/sleeping) ·
05 Room Expansions (library, garden, observatory, bedroom; layouts linked
digital + physical) · 06 Expressive Mechanisms (doors, shadows, rocker).
Buy a £20 house today, upgrade it later: standardise electronics cavity,
connectors, access panels, power routing and expansion interfaces from
the start.

## Digital twin (Pogtown's part)

Physical: lighting, sound, tactile, expansions. Digital: resident, daily
activities, furnishings, artwork, animations, conversations. Atlas/Marble
for atmosphere; deterministic scene graph for furniture, activities and
owned upgrades. Buy Jenny a piano → appears digitally; commission the
miniature → fits physically. Jenny has projects (music, stories,
paintings) appearing around her room. Stable physical object, evolving
inside.

## Stonedoorway as the bedtime experience

Storytime (consistent resident voice) · dream journal (permissioned
morning note) · guided imagery (sourced practices, adapted) · breathing
light (windows as visual guide) · sleep soundscapes · morning companion
(reliable alarm + spoken routine) · memory palace (familiar rooms as
mnemonics). Practices structured, transparent, user-controlled. Bedside
foundations: physical mic mute, recording indicators, offline alarm,
adjustable brightness, no unrequested night speech.

## Canonical architecture

Pogtown resident (identity, personality, memory, voice, activities) →
persistent digital house (Atlas visuals, GLB furniture, exact scene
graph, events) → companion services (voice, alarm scheduler,
Stonedoorway practices, messaging) → OddHobb physical house
(Shenzhen shell, LED, audio, display, modular furniture). Manufacturing:
JLC3DP shell/roof/windows/furniture · JLCPCB/Elecrow/Makerfabs
electronics + firmware · Seeed/M5Stack early modules · Shenzhen
consolidation (house + electronics + booklet + accessories, one gift).

## First product: Living House 001, Jenny's Cottage

Palm-sized cottage, translucent windows, replaceable roof, fixed
electronics cavity, unique digital identity. Prototype: warm LED,
speaker, microphone, Wi-Fi microcontroller; phone/desktop app handles
character + voice; house takes authorised light/audio commands. Even a
passive printed cottage + nearby phone tests most of it. First demo:
bedtime story in her voice, windows dim, digital room shows her reading
by the fire — and she remembers the page tomorrow night. Longer term:
persistent companion with a home to hold, furnish, illuminate, expand.
Every voice/world/AR/robotics advance upgrades the same house.
