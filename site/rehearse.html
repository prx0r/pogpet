Prepare a reusable template

Record the action, extract its movement, retarget to your standard rig, and clean feet, hands and prop contact. Export a self-contained animated GLB. Then run the motion inspector in your checkout to create the draft asset manifest.

python3 scripts/inspect_motion.py putt.glb
python3 scripts/inspect_motion.py putt.glb \
  --clip 0 --rig-id oddhobb-humanoid-v1 --poster-ms 1800

The exported poster is a rehearsal image. Customer print artwork still needs the card layout, text and print resolution. Uploaded customer likenesses are not automatically attached to this performance.
