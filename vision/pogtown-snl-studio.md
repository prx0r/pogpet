# Autonomous animation studio: Pogtown as synthetic SNL (founder, verbatim 2026-10-10)

> Endgame: recurring characters, writers' room, directors, pipeline,
> audience research. Stand-up → 2–5-min sketches is a small jump with
> reusable performers; sketches → movies reuses the machinery. Own the
> fictional world, not the video generator.

## Formats: topical news satire, character sitcoms, fake ads (OddHobb
## tie-in), animated films. Persistent personalities, histories, voices,
## designs, relationships — performances generated from structured identity.

## Character theory drives comedy (Nolan cybercrime sketch: setup →
## escalation → character error → consequence). Sketch = Event + Characters
## + JokeBlocks + SceneGraph. Measure which combinations resonate.

## Autonomous production: news/culture research (verified sources,
## factual premise, satirisable contradictions) → writers' room
## (graph + blocks + Jev treatments, competing scripts) → director
## (shot list, motions, voices, lip sync, lighting, camera, fframes
## render) → review gates (technical QC, source verification,
## copyright/privacy, fact-vs-fiction separation, authorisation) →
## distribution + learning (retention/shares/comments/repeat →
## hypotheses → next production). News comedy keeps human approval for
## higher-risk publication. Every episode = FinalBuilds2 build with
## provenance, acceptance tests, experiment ID, observations.

## Reusable motion vocabulary (not per-sentence generation): speaking
## (assert/whisper/explain/interrupt/shout), reactions, physical comedy
## (fall/recoil/trip/freeze/turn), social, timing (pause/double
## take/stare/reveal) — combined with voice timing, gaze, expressions,
## transitions. ARDY/Kimodo/retargeting extend vocabulary. Voice is
## tractable; hard part is multi-performer dialogue timing.

## Ladder: 15–60s joke (timing/hooks) → 1–3-min sketch (multi-character)
## → 5–10-min sitcom (narrative/staging) → 20-min episode (structure/
## consistency) → 90-min film (plot/arcs/continuity/editorial). Movies
## aren't 90 one-minute videos; but five-minute-sketch machinery transfers.

## Three-attempt competition: new comedians get 3 audience attempts, most
## archive; winners earn screen time, crossovers, relationships, formats
## (news desk regulars, sitcoms), ensembles → specials/movies. Measure
## repeat interest, recognition, retention, cross-story chemistry. A
## fictional network evolves from feedback, full history retained.

## Start: Pogtown News — recurring 2–3-min animated comedy, one set, two
## hosts, guests, fixed cameras, reusable motions. Research finds stories,
## writers satirise, production generates, Influence distributes,
## FinalBuilds2 evaluates. Fixed format, bounded cost, infinite variation.
## Then sketches, fake ads, sitcoms, game shows, specials. Start producing
## NOW, simple animation — theory improves fastest with real reactions.
