   bundle: a card plus its relevant video add-on. That bundle is a new
   concept — the **gift pack** — not a reason to merge the tabs.

   will like, ideally already customised.
3. **Endgame:** never leave the page. Voice-customise with Oddy, 3D-create
   the card or jibbit or whatever inside that product's constraints, build
   a gift pack to the budget, check out with Shopify.

### Third batch (2026-10-06 — answered by best judgment)

1. **Coats stop at 40mm.** Minis below that are mesh-only; fur colour is mud
   at jibbit scale. Already true in config; now the rule.
2. **Oddy asks, never truncates.** A name that won't fit its zone comes back
   with `needs_shorten` and no text — Oddy gets a shorter form or initials
   from the human instead of silently chopping.
3. **Mesh first, relief as the cheaper tier.** The friend's mesh is the gift;
   relief-with-text is the budget alternative, offered second.
4. **Matcher gets built.** Asset-filters part 3: every upload scored against
   every line, cards-only gallery today becomes shelf-wide.
5. **Mesh-origin exceptions approved.** The five face_swap lines are mesh by
   nature, not violations of reference-first — the rule governs functional
   geometry, and their functional geometry is the loop, ring, pin and
   footprint, all locked.

## Reaction

Gift pack as Oddy's game — best gift for the cheapest price inside a budget
— is the sharpest version yet. It turns the whole shelf into her instrument
instead of a list. Reference-first as the guarantee makes the design space
honest: lock the dimensions that make the thing work, free everything else,
mesh-led design happens inside that safety. And perform as the viral wedge
is the right home for pogtown: the clip is the advert, the share link is
the funnel, starter meshes are the hook, first upload is conversion. The
prompt saved with the script is the detail that makes sets replayable and
shareable. Building all three now: pack composer, reference-first marking,
share-link funnel.
